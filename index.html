Questions

Good to know

How long does a game take?

You choose. Quick runs about an hour and a half, Classic is about two and a half to four hours, and Epic adds more rounds. The plan shows a time estimate for your group.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Your game saves automatically in the browser you plan it in. To use it on another phone or computer, open your game's share link there.

What do I need to buy?

Mostly party basics: cups, beanbags, buffs or bandanas, rocks and paint, plus a few printouts. The supply list gives exact amounts and buy links for your player count.

Can my co-host see the plan?

Yes. Tap Share link in your game and send it. They get their own copy of the plan, tribes and script on their device.

Is it really free?

Yes. Plan as many parties as you like.
